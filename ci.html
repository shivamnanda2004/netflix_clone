Corporate Information

Netflix, Inc. is an American subscription streaming service and production company.

About Netflix

Founded in 1997 by Reed Hastings and Marc Randolph, Netflix has grown from a DVD-by-mail service into the world's leading streaming entertainment platform with over 260 million paid memberships in more than 190 countries.
