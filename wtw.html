Ways to Watch Netflix

Netflix is available on hundreds of devices so you can watch wherever you are.

Smart TVs

Samsung, LG, Sony, Android TV, and more. Just download the Netflix app from your TV app store.

Streaming Devices

Apple TV, Amazon Fire TV, Chromecast, Roku, and more.

Game Consoles

PlayStation 4/5 and Xbox One/Series X|S all support Netflix.

Mobile & Tablets

Available on iOS and Android devices. Download from the App Store or Google Play.

Computers

Watch directly in your browser at netflix.com — no download required.
